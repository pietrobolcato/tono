# Tono v0.71-opt

**Sound variations.** On a layer’s SOUND page, turn PRESETS to read the name and listening cue for each variation. REC applies it. HOME cancels. Each patch has its own menu, including the A22 instruments and sample sources. For example, try Veil on Cloud Bloom for a darker background, or Open brass to bring it forward.

**Texture families.** Choose Ocean Air for eight air and noise beds, Paper Grain for six granular textures or Ocean Beads for seven percussion textures. Turn PRESETS to choose the individual sound. Bowed Sheet, Undertow, Rail Crossing and the other distinct textures still have their own entries. Existing songs recall their original texture.

**Two ways to shape frequencies.** SOUND → EDIT → Filter shapes the synth. FX → Layer Filter shapes the whole layer. On either page, choose Bell with K1, centre it with K2, boost or cut with K3 and adjust its width with K4. Start at 0 dB, then boost to bring a band forward or cut to make space. K3 controls resonance in low-pass and high-pass modes. Songs saved with Comb keep their filter. Turning TYPE away from Comb selects one of the current choices.

**Quick modulation.** Move a layer control and press HOME + LFO to link it. Successive links alternate between LFO 1 and LFO 2, independently on each layer. For example, link Density, then Warmth. A third link replaces Density on LFO 1. Each lane keeps its rate, shape and depth.

**MASTER response.** Small movements near the physical ends now produce small gain changes. If the pot reaches an end before the layer reaches the level you want, hold HOME, move MASTER back, release HOME and continue adjusting.

**Soft Knee saturation.** Open a layer’s FX → Drive and select Soft Knee. Raise DRIVE to round the peaks and bring out harmonics. TONE at its centre keeps the original colour. The effect uses the dry monitor’s saturation curve on this layer, before it joins the mix.

**Live EQ spectra.** Layer EQ shows the selected layer after EQ, while Master EQ shows the stereo output. The filled spectrum runs from low frequencies on the left to high frequencies on the right. The line shows the response of LOW, MID, HIGH and OUTPUT. On Master EQ, the display takes the stronger channel at each frequency so out-of-phase stereo content stays visible.
