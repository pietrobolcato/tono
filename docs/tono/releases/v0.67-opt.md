# Tono v0.67-opt

- Open Help with HOME + EDIT for a guide to the screen you are on. Turn SELECT for the controls and their explanations. The guide uses the same button and knob symbols as the footer.
- The Generate Interplay preview shows when layers play, take turns or leave space. Tide Together displays the full twelve-bar cycle, with eight bars of shared density changes and four bars of rest.
- Hold HOME on a selected layer for Attack, the existing role-specific K2 control, Mid EQ and Drive. Drums and one-shot samples get Low EQ in place of Attack.
- Footer control symbols light in the theme’s accent when you use the matching buttons and knobs.
