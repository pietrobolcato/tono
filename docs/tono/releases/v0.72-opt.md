# Tono v0.72-opt

**Filter slope.** On SOUND → EDIT → Filter or FX → Layer Filter, select LP or HP and turn K4. At the same cutoff, 6 dB/oct leaves more of the sound audible beyond the cutoff, 12 dB/oct rolls it off more firmly, and 24 dB/oct gives the steepest slope. Bell uses K4 for width. Songs from earlier releases keep their original filter response when recalled.

**Layer tremolo.** Open a layer's FX page and turn SELECT to Tremolo. K1 sets rate, K2 sets depth, K3 selects sine, triangle, saw or square, and K4 sets left/right phase. At 0° both sides swell together. At 180° a sine swell moves from side to side. Depth starts at Off. Tremolo settings save with the song and both LFO slots stay available for other controls.

**Abstract Rain.** Choose Abstract Rain in the Texture sound bank, then turn PRESETS for Chill Rain, Steady Downpour or Rain & Thunder. REC applies the selected weather. Earlier texture patches and their stored IDs are preserved.

**Cloud Bloom.** In the Pad bank, choose Cloud Bloom and turn PRESETS. Original is the familiar pad. Still Water gives a smooth sine bed, Twin Reeds adds a slow beat between two triangle oscillators, and Glass Tide brings a soft upper fifth in and out. Press REC to apply. Still Water, Twin Reeds and Glass Tide replace Veil and Open brass in this patch’s picker.
