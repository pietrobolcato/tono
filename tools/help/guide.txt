# Groups occupy consecutive entries. Each line is one card.
[home]
Choose Master to shape the whole mix, or a layer to shape one instrument. The level pot follows the selected target.
Hold HOME for Attack or Low EQ, the role shortcut, Mid EQ and Drive. Release HOME to return to the main controls.
[ensemble]
Read each layer's peak after its gain. NOTE is held, REL is fading and TAIL is a sound handoff. REST can still leave echoes.
[zen]
Notes animate the landscape. The transport moves the scene and travel changes its colours. The mix controls still work here.
[mode]
Choose what this layer plays. A new role starts its own automatic pattern and keeps the layer level.
[sound]
Browse patches silently before applying one. The four controls shape the current patch immediately.
Set the oscillator or engine colours before the layer filter and effects.
Shape the filter response. Listen for the edge around its cutoff as you add resonance.
Set how this layer plays its notes and where it sits in stereo.
Balance the drum voices inside this kit before changing the whole layer level.
[sample]
Choose a recording and trim its start and end to play just the part you want.
Set the playback mode, tuning and loop. Smooth the join if repeats click.
Shape the loop edge, filter, stereo motion and room around the recording.
Record an incoming USB audio stream into this layer's take. Finish the take before editing it.
[env]
Shape a note from its first attack to the tail after release. Try a longer attack for a swell.
Shape each percussion hit. Short hits leave room between steps.
Trim the sample and soften its edges to avoid abrupt starts or stops.
[lfo]
Move a destination around its saved value. Choose the group, then the destination. Raise depth to hear it.
Set a steady cycle or let each cycle pick a speed between two limits.
[pattern]
Build phrases from the chord and scale. Set how often notes arrive and how densely the layer plays.
Generate a drum part. Swing delays offbeats and fills add hits near the end of a phrase.
Choose a motion for the noise bed. Steady holds a continuous sound.
Place notes or rests one step at a time. Each note has its own velocity.
Choose a drum lane and toggle its hits on the step keys. Other lanes keep their own hits.
[fx]
Push this layer into distortion. Choose a circuit and use tone to tame the bright edge.
Add chorus for width or sweep the layer across the stereo field.
Feed this layer into the shared echo. Its delay algorithm affects every layer using that bus.
Feed this layer into the shared room. A higher send leaves more reverb after each note.
Place the layer left or right or move it repeatedly with auto-pan.
Overlap small pieces of the layer's recent sound. Blend them into the dry signal for a moving texture.
[eq]
Boost or cut lows, mids and highs on this layer. Trim its output if a boost makes it too loud.
Adjust the balance of lows, mids and highs across the whole mix. Trim the output after boosting a band.
[global]
Set how strongly each aspect of the piece drifts. The interval spaces automatic chord changes.
Set the root, available notes and tempo for generated parts. Changing scale replaces the note pool.
Rhythm sets when notes land, phrase sets their path and interplay sets who plays together. Style changes land every four bars during play.
Choose the screen palette. The same controls and sounds are available in each theme.
Choose how the song changes as you travel. Tail time and handoff shape the switch between sounds.
[masterfx]
Set the shared room for all reverb sends. Size changes its space and damping softens its tail.
Set the shared repeats. Time spaces the echoes and feedback determines how long they continue.
Set chorus movement and stereo width across the mix. Reverb modulation moves the room tail.
Colour the full mix with filter and saturation. Delay colour and modulation shape its echoes.
[timing]
Set the unit and shortest and longest wait between moves. Equal limits give a regular pulse. Reach limits each new destination.
Rotate generator families or choose a whole preset. Follow Travel shares its deadline; a separate clock uses the two limits.
Replace one eligible layer at a time. Follow Travel shares its deadline; a separate clock uses the two limits.
[modal]
Recall a factory song for a fresh take or a user slot for its stored arrangement. During play it enters at the next bar.
Save a snapshot of the current song in a user slot. During play the snapshot writes when playback stops.
The slot already has a save. Confirm to replace it, or cancel to keep its contents.
Restore this layer's sound, mix and pattern from the last loaded piece. Other layers keep playing.
Keep the role to change its sound, or replace the role and start an automatic pattern. During play the change lands next bar.
Browse settings for this page. Applying a choice changes this group of controls together.
Save the four generator settings together for recall in another song.
Preview a new chord before changing the harmony of the generated layers.
Preview a new role before replacing this layer's instrument and automatic pattern.
Preview the proposed sound. Applying it replaces this layer's patch and lets the old sound release.
