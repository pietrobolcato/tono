# Tono control card

The playable instrument is the real `.fwsc` package in Joakim Eriksson's FM1 hardware emulator. The handoff HTML is a visual specification. Use one sounding instance at low volume; preserve unsaved emulator work before switching packages.

## Interaction pattern for new features

Proposals own the display above the navigation footer. Show the proposed result there and hide the underlying page’s controls. HOME + ALGORITHM shows only the two actions for the selected layer. Turn ALGORITHM to keep its type and change its sound, or to change its type. REC triggers the Auto choice, at the next bar during playback.

On every screen with a footer, lay out shortcuts from left to right in reading order. Fill the first line with as many complete commands as fit, then continue on the second. Keep each control, button combination and action label together. Count the cap padding and middle dot when measuring the available space. SEL changes the main page. SELECT moves within a page. HOME + EDIT opens contextual Help, SELECT browses the shortcut guide and each assigned knob, and HOME closes it.

- **EDIT opens detail in place.** Keep the familiar controls on the main page. Name the detail action in the footer, use EDIT to return, and explain each changed knob in HOME + EDIT. For a bounded range, show units, let either endpoint meet the other, and clamp the endpoint being turned.
- **SELECT navigates** the current screen, such as a layer, module, detail group or LFO category. **HOME ALGORITHM travels live through the song**. On the relevant editing pages **ALGORITHM proposes a replacement** chord, role, patch or sample source. Continue turning ALGORITHM to browse. Describe proposed sounds by what someone would hear, using auditioned descriptions for factory sounds.
- **PRESETS belongs to the current page.** HOME offers songs. SOUND offers three variations of the selected patch. ADSR envelope, LFO, each layer and master FX module, and both EQ pages have local settings. Pages without a bank ignore PRESETS. A local preset changes only its page's settings. LFO presets keep the destination.

On HOME, PRESETS starts at Garden. Turn forward for Nostalgic New Inn, Last Tram, First Ferry, Berlin Mornings, Acid Konichiwa, Window Light, Iceland Memories, Symphony for Mars, Tales of Sand Dunes, Tales of Cold Desert Dunes, Symphony for a Warmer Mars, 303 Fellows, Analog Days Quartet, and Analog Days Warm. Quartet pairs Wurli and felt piano with linen pad and bass. Warm pairs the same two keys with analog brass and sub bass. Factory proposals include a short sound description. Any saved user slots follow the factory songs.
- **REC commits, HOME cancels.** Browsing a proposed change is silent. Numbered knobs are direct performance or parameter edits. Retain dedicated actions such as REC for pattern entry and SAVE for storage. Add contextual help, a pending REC light, and tests for browsing, cancellation and commit with each new proposal.

- HOME SELECT: MASTER → L1 → L2 → L3 → L4. Four sound layers; MASTER adds no voice. The MASTER pot follows the last HOME target. In EDIT → SOUND it follows the layer being edited. The current pot position anchors the saved gain. The remaining travel left maps to silence and the remaining travel right maps to full gain. Returning to the anchor restores its gain. At power-on the pot establishes its reference position and Garden opens at its authored master gain. Selecting a target with the knob still keeps the saved level. While changing a selected layer's gain, the top HOME label/value/bar temporarily shows that layer; MASTER returns after 1.2 seconds without an accepted gain change.
- HOME / Master K2 controls **Density**. At 100% the song plays as authored. Turning down skips more sequencer attacks across layers. At 0% no new sequenced attacks start. The clock, live keys and release tails continue. Hold HOME and turn K2 for the original Echo macro. Density saves with the song. To reopen a save on 0.54 or earlier, save a copy at 100% density.
- HOME knobs: displayed performance macros, bounded offsets from deep baselines. Pad/Drone knob 3 is SUSTAIN, affecting envelope, hold length and generated activity. New factory Pad/Drone patches have sustained envelopes and 84% base activity. Existing user envelopes are retained.
- HOME shows battery bars and a lightning bolt beside them when it detects an active USB host. The bolt is an external-power indicator.
- HOME keyboard: shared pitch-class pool. Bright keys are chord tones, softer lights are available pool notes. Tap to add/remove classes for generation; tonic stays and at least three classes remain. A stock scale replaces the custom pool; key transposes it. EDIT's keyboard can otherwise audition literal/off-scale notes, except on PATTERN or while its sound is being replaced.
- EDIT opens the selected layer (MASTER opens GLO MASTER FX). Seven pages: MODE, SOUND, ENVELOPE, LFO, PATTERN, FX, LAYER EQ. Physical **SCL** is the handoff's **SEL** page button.
- MODE ALGORITHM previews a layer type. REC commits the same type change as Auto Travel and HOME layer evolution. The incoming layer starts on AUTO in its patch register and keeps its mix level. Its layer EQ and grains come from the loaded arrangement for that role, or start flat and dry when the role is absent. Turning ALGORITHM alone is silent. EDIT opens SOUND. HOME + SAVE on MODE proposes restoring this track to its last loaded sound, macros, pattern, level and layer EQ; REC confirms, HOME cancels. Other layers and global settings remain.
- **LFO → EDIT** opens Rate settings. K1 selects Fixed, Rnd Hz or Rnd Bars. Fixed gives K2 its usual Hz and tempo divisions. In either random mode K2 sets the minimum and K3 the maximum. Bars are whole four-beat bars, 1–16, initially 1–4. Each cycle picks a duration and follows tempo changes while it runs. EDIT returns to the waveform page, where K2 shows the live rate or bar count and shifts both limits together. Shape, depth, SELECT category, K4 destination and LFO presets belong to the waveform page. Rate ranges are song settings. For recall on firmware through 0.53, save a separate copy with all layers set to Fixed. The 0.54-dev preview also requires changing Rnd Bars before recall.
- On SOUND / PATCH, ALGORITHM proposes a patch. The proposal shows the current and proposed names and a listening description of the destination factory patch. A personal take has no factory description. REC applies the patch with the usual release tail. HOME cancels. EDIT toggles PATCH/DETAIL; DETAIL SELECT cycles ENGINE/FILTER/PLAY. Ordinary parameter knobs remain direct. On SAMPLE / SOURCE, ALGORITHM proposes and REC applies a PCM source. EDIT opens Playback, SELECT switches Playback / Character, and EDIT returns to Source. MIC TAKE enables USB capture.
- Patch, sample-source, role and restore replacements use **GLO / TRAVEL / knob 2 = TAIL TIME**, 250–30000 ms in 250 ms steps, default 3000. This now sets the duration for active short and long synth releases. Current amplitude falls smoothly; FM6 internal envelopes are held while oscillators continue. In RELEASE mode the incoming patch enters after the outgoing envelope finishes. In CROSSFADE mode it can enter while the release is still audible. Already-ended hits/one-shots cannot be stretched from silence. Rapid choices coalesce without restarting the old release. Pending state is shown in the footer; edits to the queued sound apply on arrival. Realtime overload protection can override tails under stress.
- Whole-piece recalls use one coordinated transition: outgoing sources, master, EQ and FX settings remain until release completion; then the destination starts. The existing shared reverb engine may reset its own model buffers when the new piece selects a different effect model. No duplicate master effect is rendered.
- The former global GLIDE shortcut is now TAIL TIME; individual GLIDE remains in SOUND/DETAIL/PLAY. Normal AUTO chords and key/scale/pool edits keep their normal note envelopes/common-tone holds. Explicit queued GENERATE style changes release generated parts at their four-bar boundary; manual patterns remain literal. No generator-imposed pitch slide or automatic per-chord volume fader is added.
- GLO EVOLVE ALGORITHM proposes a compatible chord degree. REC commits immediately while stopped or at the next bar during PLAY. It does not change tonic/key. EVOLVE SELECT chooses OFF/1/2/4/8/16/32/64 automatic bars. The seeded degree bag avoids immediate/A–B–A repeats; eight PHRASES also vary Pad motifs and six voicing pairs.
- GLO GENERATE knobs 1–3 choose 9 rhythms, 9 phrases, 6 interplay policies after a 90 ms final settle. Knob 4 sets variation. During PLAY, style changes queue together to the four-bar grid; choosing the active style cancels its pending change. Turn PRESETS to browse factory generator settings. Garden Original recalls the factory Garden rhythm, Amb Breathe, Blend and 35% variation. Open Garden uses 15%. Each proposal describes its phrasing and mood. REC applies rhythm, phrase, interplay and variation together, immediately when stopped or at the next four-bar downbeat during playback. HOME cancels a preview. Turning a Generate parameter directly supersedes a queued preset. SAVE captures the applied settings with the song. Style commits use the selected timed transition for generated sources. Manual notes/patches remain literal.
- LFO dot follows the real DSP phase, at the chosen frequency/shape; S&H uses its actual held value. Fast oscillation is sampled at display refresh. FX graphics show the actual send/routing or a control-response diagram, not an audio meter or measured spectrum.
- On pitched layers, PATTERN REC captures the generated 16-step phrase and arms silent entry. Use SELECT to choose a step and press any white or black key to write its pitch there. The cursor stays on that step. The keyboard softly lights in-scale pitches and highlights the note at the cursor. Knobs 3 and 4 edit pitch and velocity. ARP clears the selected step. On Drums, SELECT chooses a lane and the 16 white keys toggle its steps directly. Keys write silently, including during playback. REC ends entry and preserves the manual pattern.
- PATTERN's **amber marker / PLAY number** is the actual layer step, respecting SPEED and swing dispatch, including rests. It is a clock position, not a note-on/audio-level meter; envelopes and echoes sound between steps. The **thin green marker / EDIT number** is the selected entry slot. The page updates at up to 20 Hz. EDIT or SEQ again toggles MANUAL/AUTO without deleting manual edits; toggle back to reuse them. REC from AUTO captures a fresh phrase.
- LAYER EQ is one SCL press after FX. LOW/MID/HIGH/OUTPUT each cover −12…+12 dB. Broad fixed splits are about 180 Hz / 2.3 kHz. Processing precedes dry mix and shared FX sends, including drum image/per-lane sends. Each track has independent state/gains; flat EQ is exact bypass, changes smooth, high boosts have a bounded headroom knee. Master EQ remains separate.
- Texture: fresh patches use sustained NOISE, 100% activity and AUTO STEADY mode, which holds a continuous voice regardless of sparse conductor events. PULSE/DRIFT/BURSTS/SPARSE modes allow gated texture activity. Sound changes reset fresh Texture recipes to STEADY. High Air and Distant Hiss replace the pitched Glass Wind/Distant Voices factory recipes. Saved old PHASE patches retain their original names/parameters; choose a fresh Texture patch for the revised palette.
- HOME PRESETS browses the factory bank followed by recallable user slots. After a confirmed choice the knob continues from that position. REC recalls at the next bar during PLAY; HOME cancels. Replacing sounding tracks uses the bounded old-release handoff. Recall does not save.
- SAVE: SELECT chooses a destination. SAVED slots can be recalled, EMPTY slots are free, and UNREADABLE slots need explicit overwrite. REC captures the current piece. During playback the snapshot waits in RAM and writes automatically on STOP. Wait for SONG SAVED before closing the emulator or powering down. A second REC confirms overwriting an occupied slot. Failed writes retain the previous CRC-verified A/B copy. Unsupported flash is refused.
- On HOME, select a layer and hold HOME while tapping PLAY to lock or unlock its sound. A padlock marks locked layers. Travel skips the layer’s patch, role and sound macros. Its notes keep playing and you can edit the sound yourself. Saving the song remembers the locks. After unlocking, the next eligible Travel move can change the sound.
- HOME held + SELECT in EDIT changes the edited layer on the same page; the volume target stays at the last HOME choice. HOME + OCT− toggles mute for the current layer (the edited layer in EDIT); HOME + OCT+ solos/unsolos it.
- Emulator combinations: hold either computer **Shift** and press **Z** (or click OCT−) to toggle the current layer’s mute. For other combinations hold computer **7** (HOME), then **Z** (OCT−) / **X** (OCT+), or click the corresponding panel button while holding 7. Hold 7 plus left/right for cross-layer editing. The mouse alone holds one control at a time.
- PLAY tap starts/stops; hold 800 ms for emergency output fade. A later tap reopens the output. ENV/LFO/FX/SEQ/ARP are layer shortcuts; During PLAY, OCT± queues a lasting octave shift at the next downbeat for the selected layer (all four at Master). The shift stays through later bars and STOP/PLAY. Each tap moves one octave, up to ±3 per layer. Tap the opposite direction to move back. Q+1 means one octave up is queued, O+1 means active. On HOME and ENSEMBLE you can queue the first bar before PLAY. In a stopped editor OCT± shifts the audition keyboard. LEDs indicate available actions; pending REC confirmations breathe, recording REC stays solid.
- The bottom bar shows the contextual shortcut on the left and HOME + EDIT for Help on the right. Travel timing, sound detail and proposal confirmations use this bar. Transient notices appear above it. Fullscreen Zen keeps its wordmark-only layout.

## Automatic travel

GLO → TRAVEL (page 2/7) has an explicit AUTO switch on knob 4. EDIT opens or closes TRAVEL / TIMING. SCL from either screen continues to HARMONY. SELECT on the Travel page chooses Long Journeys (the original reversible travel), New Chapters or Branching Paths. In TIMING knob 1 chooses Bars, Seconds, Minutes or Hours independently of AUTO. Knobs 2 and 3 set the shortest and longest wait (1–120). Set both to the same value for regular movement. Knob 4 sets REACH (1–80 detents), the maximum distance of a new destination from the current position. GLO → TRAVEL still sets Bounded or Endless. Long Journeys advances one or two clicks toward a destination and lingers there for one interval. New Chapters moves one or two clicks at a time. Every four clicks of total travel, a different layer takes a new timbre and the other three carry over. Branching Paths can change direction en route. Every three clicks it chooses an anchor layer and new sounds for the other three. During playback at most one instrument changes per downbeat. Manual turns follow the selected style even with AUTO Off. A HOME ALGORITHM turn takes priority and starts a fresh wait. Stopping suspends automatic movement. SAVE stores the Travel style, switch, handoff, reach and timing lanes in the selected user song. Factory starters and older songs use the factory Travel defaults.

## What follows a saved song

SAVE → choose USER 1–8 → REC captures the mix, master and layer gains, layer patches and parameters, EQ, effects, patterns, harmony, generator and Travel controls. It also captures each layer’s applied octave, mute, solo and sound locks, the Zen landscape choice, and FX bypass. A song recalled during playback takes over at the next bar. SAVE while playing captures the settings at that moment and writes them when playback stops.

Theme applies across the instrument. Selecting a factory song loads its authored arrangement. To keep an edit to a factory song, save a user copy. Songs from earlier versions can be read, including embedded short takes. To use an older firmware again, retain the original backup, since the older reader lacks the new settings extension.

## Zen landscapes

Hold **HOME + ARP** to open Zen. **SCL** switches between Contour and Train Ride. Tap **HOME** to return. SELECT, MASTER, the four macros, ALGORITHM travel, PRESETS and transport retain their HOME actions. The control LEDs go dark while the landscape fills the 240 × 240 display with the Tono wordmark in the corner, fixed white in TE Dark and black in both light themes. HOME + EDIT opens contextual help. Real synth onsets make contours pulse and train-window glints appear. Envelopes shape contour sizes and ridge heights. Travel changes palette, contour spacing, horizon height, relief and weather. The transport clocks the landscape, including dunes, mountains, town streets, working quays, sea, woodland and viaducts, passing foreground objects and the day/night cycle.

## Choir, ensemble and help

- In EDIT → SOUND → PATCH, turn ALGORITHM past the original eight Pad or Drone sounds. Pad 9–11 are Open Aah, Round Ooh and Plainchant. Drone 9–11 are Low Ooh, Chapel Aah and Cantus. These use JIANT VOICE. The four SOUND controls shape the starting vowel, glottal buzz, breath and formant shift. EDIT opens the second vowel, morph time, resonance and randomness. On HOME, VOWEL offsets the starting vowel.
- The new PRESETS entries follow Night Garden. Window Seats is a 58 BPM D-major piano miniature with Ooh, chip drone and harp. Stone Cloister is a D-Dorian vocal line over a low Ooh pedal with harp and air. Both leads start in AUTO. On Stone Cloister the Pad PATTERN mode CHANT generates a new contour for each phrase, with a reciting tone, neighboring notes and a cadence. Factory recall starts a fresh take. A saved user piece keeps its seed and any recorded MANUAL steps. Tidal Loom pairs evolving FLOAT synthesis with Porcelain and reed bass in F Lydian. Kite Strings is an A-major harp and soft organ piece with a Euclidean pulse.
- Press SCL on HOME for ENSEMBLE. HOME held + SEQ also opens it from another page. Each row shows that layer's post-level mixer peak and voice state. NOTE is a gated voice, REL is its release, TAIL is a pending sound change, and REST is an idle source. Shared reverb and echoes can continue during REST. Knobs 1–4 directly mix L1–L4. SCL or HOME returns to HOME.
- Hold HOME and press EDIT for the current page's knob guide. In the desktop emulator, hold **7** and click **EDIT** on the panel. Tap HOME to return to the same page. The four encoders do not edit while reading help. PLAY still controls transport.
- On manual pitched PATTERN, SELECT inspects a step. Knobs 2–4 control SPEED, NOTE and VELOCITY; knob 1 is unassigned. A positive NOTE turn on a rest inserts a note from the current voicing. ARP clears it. For manual Drums the knobs select step and lane, set a hit or rest and adjust swing. In AUTO STEADY Texture the controls are mode, noise colour, drift and filter cutoff.
- Drum ENV exposes hit decay, tone, FM attack and noise. Its SOUND → DETAIL → PLAY knobs mix the four sequenced drum voices. Sample ONE-SHOT ENV exposes start, end, gain and edge fade. Analog FX cutoff edits the synth's own filter. On a generated chord the PLAY group offers chorus amount alongside glide, transpose and pan.

- Departure Hall follows Kite Strings in PRESETS. It opens at 54 BPM in C major using C, D, E, G and A. Two felt pianos play in different registers on five- and eleven-step cycles. Round Ooh and Cedar Triangle enter on their own cycles. GLO → GENERATE starts on ODD LOOPS. HOME's lit keys edit the pitch pool for all four parts.
- In a Pad layer's SOUND page, ALGORITHM proposes Glass Haze at position 4. Amber Brass, Cedar Triangle and Umber Pulse are positions 14–16. On HOME, WARMTH closes the low-pass filter as you turn it up. On SOUND, DETUNE sets the oscillator separation and CUT opens the filter directly.
- Old user slots containing FM Glass Haze play those saved parameters. To hear the revised sound, select a neighboring Pad patch and turn back. Load the factory Stone Cloister to hear the new chant. A previously saved manual performance still plays its original notes.

Save **v4 is 1420 bytes**, within the existing 3840-byte A/B capacity and unchanged eight-slot sector bounds. v1–v3 1400-byte records migrate with flat layer EQ and a 3000 ms cap. Older pre-v4 firmware cannot read v4; v0.5 may reject new Noise Texture recipes 2/4. New recipe indices above 7 also require v0.7. The new Pad IDs, revised Glass Haze and CHANT mode require this checkpoint to recall reliably. Preserve old pieces/exports and separate emulator state for rollback.

The pinned HAL/emulator model knobs 1–4 as quadrature encoders; only MASTER is an absolute ADC pot. No analog wiring or external input was invented. The generic absolute selector decoder remains separately tested.

## Garden and Tono sounds

- Select **Garden** with PRESETS and confirm with REC. It starts in D Dorian at 72 BPM. In another piece, choose GLO → GENERATE → RHYTHM → GARDEN to use its timing and pitch choices with that piece’s current layers.
- On EDIT → SOUND, Pad has Garden Rhodes, Sunroom Keys and Linen Pad. Drone has Skylight. Bells has Garden Bell, Willow Tine and Dew Bell. Bass has Garden Pedal and Linden Bass. Drums has Garden Pulse. Turn ALGORITHM on PATCH and press REC to switch sounds.
- In GLO → MASTER FX choose PLATE on the reverb bank or SHORT ECHO, TWO HEAD or ECHO+TAPE on the delay bank. The layer sends and shared return controls set how much you hear.

HOME Master SPACE changes room size, width and layer sends. K2 DENSITY controls how often sequenced notes sound. Hold HOME and turn K2 for ECHO, which changes return level, repeats and sends. MOTION couples chorus, reverb modulation and stereo movement. WARMTH rounds attacks, adds drive and darkens overtones and repeats. Each piece starts from its saved mix. Layer controls use the patch's own synthesis engine and role. On vocal patches VOWEL sweeps the formant, and SAMPLE retains its dedicated pitch control.

On EDIT → LFO, SELECT jumps between categories and knob 4 browses destinations inside the selected category. For Open Aah choose Instrument → BREATH. For grain amount choose Grain → MIX. Both controls route immediately. Raise DEPTH to hear the movement. Continuous parameters move around the edited value. Pattern changes take effect at their next scheduled event, and note modulation preserves rests. Each layer stores its own route. Changing patch loads the new patch’s default route.

While editing a layer, turn a parameter knob and then hold **HOME** and tap **LFO** to open LFO with that parameter linked. The shortcut keeps the layer’s waveform, rate and depth. Each layer remembers its own last knob edit until its sound is replaced or restored. If the last edited control has no LFO destination, the current destination stays selected and the screen shows a notice.

GLO → TRAVEL uses knob 3 to switch between CROSSFADE (default) and RELEASE. TAIL TIME spans 0.25–30 seconds in quarter-second steps. Crossfade uses two existing mixer parts for outgoing releases. When both are occupied, further layers retain their releases on their original parts. One shared FX section processes the mix. The mode is a device preference and the duration belongs to the piece.

EDIT → FX → GRANULAR uses MIX, LENGTH, PITCH and SCATTER. Two overlapping grains read the layer's recent audio. Texture GRAIN and Sample BLUR bring it up most strongly. Bells SHIMMER and Pad/Drone SPACE add a smaller amount above their centre. Master MOTION adds a little grain to bells, textures and samples. During SOUND edits MASTER adjusts the edited layer on the first physical turn. HOME shows an orange MASTER label when its four knobs control the full mix.

New acoustic patches are Solo Violin, Chamber Cello, Tenor Sax and Concert Flute under Pad, Bowed Cello under Drone and Kawai Grand under Bells. All five recordings are also available as Sample sources. Shore Wash, Rain on Tin, Ember Bed and Window Draft join Texture. They use the continuous foundation pattern.

### Compare dry and processed sound

Hold HOME and tap FX while playing. The footer shows **FX BYPASSED**. Repeat the shortcut to hear the processed mix again. The dry monitor takes each voice before layer drive, granular, EQ, auto-pan and shared effects. It retains synthesis, envelopes, pitch modulation, level, pan, solo and output protection. Effects keep running during the comparison. SAVE captures this monitoring choice with the song. In fm1-live hold **7** and tap **1**. **F3** records the monitored output.

On LAYER EQ and GLOBAL EQ, the three segmented bars show live input levels below approximately 180 Hz, between 180 Hz and 3 kHz, and above 3 kHz. The line and orange points show the selected EQ gains. The Master display measures both channels.

On HOME, turn ALGORITHM left for a closer, darker and more driven mix or right for a clearer, more spacious one. Each song starts at position 0 with 18 detents on either side. Colour and sends move progressively during playback. Harmony changes on a downbeat and the generator keeps running. At wider intervals, layers can change instruments one at a time, using GLO release/crossfade. Turn back to revisit a colour. Manual steps and velocities stay intact as their instruments change. A hand-picked patch or sample becomes that layer’s new return sound. SAVE stores the current arrangement. Recalling it starts a fresh landscape from there.

The Texture role includes ten recorded-grain beds. Select one on EDIT → SOUND with ALGORITHM and REC. STEADY holds it continuously; recorded manual rests release it. Ocean Beads and Undertow use an ocean drum, Cabasa Silk and Wire Brush a cabasa, Wood Rasp and Dry Timber a guiro, Seed Rain a shaker, Clockwork and Distant Cogs a ratchet, and Bowed Sheet a suspended cymbal.

### Live travel and space

Fresh settings use Branching Paths, Endless and Auto On. Movement waits 4–16 bars with Reach 32. New factory arrangements use a six-second tail with Crossfade selected on a fresh launch. Patterns and Layer types are off. Saved preferences and user-song tails retain their values.

On HOME each ALGORITHM turn shows the signed position and colour for 1.2 seconds. The centre tick marks the loaded arrangement. GLO → THEME uses SELECT for the display theme. GLO → TRAVEL uses knob 1 for travel, knob 2 for tail time and knob 3 for crossfade or release. BOUNDED keeps the original 37 positions. ENDLESS lets you keep turning on either side. Beyond the former endpoints, harmony and orchestration keep evolving within the dark or bright palette. SAVE stores this setting with the song.

On a layer’s FX → DELAY page, knob 1 sets that layer’s send and knob 4 chooses the shared echo type. GLO → MASTER FX → DELAY also controls its time and feedback. PING PONG alternates channels. SLAPBACK keeps repeats short with low feedback. The delay and reverb diagrams sketch the response to an impulse. PAN shows the smoothed position actually applied to the sounding layer.

In **GLO → TRAVEL → EDIT**, turn **SELECT** between **Movement**, **Patterns** and **Layer types**. Movement sets the automatic travel clock and reach. Patterns and Layer types each have four knobs: **Active**, **Clock**, **Min** and **Max**. Both lanes are optional. A new installation starts with both off and Follow Travel selected. Existing Journey and Move tick settings become Follow Travel.

| Clock | When the lane changes |
|---|---|
| Follow Travel | Shares the exact Auto Travel deadline, including the random wait. When AUTO is off the lane waits. Manual turns restart the Auto Travel timer. Linked changes land on a downbeat. |
| Bars / Seconds / Minutes / Hours | Runs independently during playback, including when Auto Movement is off. Min and Max accept 1–120. Equal values give a fixed interval. A range chooses another wait each time. Time-based changes land on the first downbeat after the wait. Stop and restart begin a fresh interval. |

Patterns changes one generator family at a time, cycling Rhythm, Phrase and Interplay. A manually queued choice takes priority for its family. Layer types changes at most one instrument per event using the layer’s existing release or crossfade. It introduces a role missing from the ensemble and keeps an audible Pad or Drone as a harmonic foundation. A replacement starts an AUTO pattern in its patch register. Layer EQ and grain settings come from the loaded arrangement when it contains the incoming role. An absent role starts with flat EQ and dry grains. Muted layers, protected layers, personal takes and layers in a handoff are ineligible for replacement. Solo pauses role replacement. These rules apply to Garden too.

Auto Travel skips sound, macro and role changes for the layer whose EDIT screen is open. It can evolve that layer at the next eligible change after you leave EDIT.

### Recording the stereo mix over USB

Connect the FM-1 directly to the Mac with a USB data cable while running Tono. In QuickTime Player, open **File → New Audio Recording** and choose **TONO** as the input (**JIANT** on earlier builds). Start playback on the FM-1, check the recording meter, then record and save. In a DAW choose the same device as the audio input and record stereo inputs 1/2. At 44.1 kHz the USB path sends the instrument’s stereo master mix.

### Voice sampling

On a Texture layer, browse to Eagle Landed, Radio Check or Moon Address with ALGORITHM and confirm with REC. These repeat at their recorded speed by default. The same recordings are sources in Sample.

On Sample → Sound → Source, select MIC TAKE with ALGORITHM + REC. REC now starts USB capture. Sing one held note and press REC to end early, or let the 1.44-second buffer fill. Recording starts on signal. A second REC confirmation protects an existing take. An empty input keeps the previous recording. The display shows the detected root or the C4 reference for unpitched audio. All Sample layers using MIC TAKE share that song's recording.

EDIT opens Playback. STYLE chooses keyboard tracking or ORIGINAL speed. PITCH transposes in semitones. LOOP selects Off, Forward, Reverse or Bounce. XFADE blends forward or reverse loop seams. One-shot ends at the trim boundary. The source page sets Start, End, Gain and Fade. Existing layer EQ, delay, reverb and granular controls process the sample. Save a song using MIC TAKE to store its audio inside its existing user slot.

With a layer selected on HOME, move MASTER to set its level, then press HOME + LFO to route its LFO to VOLUME. The same shortcut works after adjusting the layer level in SOUND. At full VOLUME depth the displayed crest reaches the saved level and the trough reaches silence.

### Lead and Bass

In EDIT → MODE, turn ALGORITHM to Lead and press REC. In SOUND, turn ALGORITHM to propose a voice and REC to select it. Lead plays in a melodic register and follows the song's generator. Its four performance controls are Warmth, Motion, Glide and Space. Bass includes four additional voices, with Acid Undertow and Acid Ribbon offering separate bass and lead interpretations of resonant analogue synthesis.

On HOME, select a layer. Hold HOME and turn ALGORITHM in either direction to open on **1 Keep layer / change sound**. Auto Travel proposes the next sound for this layer in the current journey. Turn ALGORITHM clockwise once for **2 Change layer**: the Layer types rule chooses a suitable role and patch for this seat, even when the timed lane is off. It considers the other roles, preserves a harmonic foundation and protects personal takes. Turn left to return to action 1. Both proposals are fixed while the menu is open. REC commits immediately when stopped or on the next bar during playback. HOME cancels. Keeping the role retains its sequence. Changing role starts an automatic pattern with the destination register, EQ and grain settings. Both preserve mixer level and pan.

On Acid Undertow and Acid Ribbon the four layer performance knobs are Cutoff, Resonance, Env Mod and Decay. In a manual pattern select a step, turn knob 1 to enable Slide In and use velocity 96 or higher for an accent. A preceding sounding note supplies the start pitch of a slide.

With GLO → GENERATE → PHRASE set to ACID LINE and a Bass or Lead pattern on AUTO, rhythm cues open short monophonic phrases. The generator takes its tonic from the current chord and snaps melodic turns into the song's note pool. A seeded motif has a recurring opening and a changing ending, then renews after four 16-step cycles. Layer speed sets the subdivision, and density controls how many spaces are filled. Try Euclidean rhythm, Acid Line phrasing and 1/16 at 96 BPM to hear the audition feel. Capturing a playing acid line into a manual pattern carries the notes, velocity accents and slide-in steps into the editor.

GLO → Travel → EDIT → Patterns: K1 CHANGE selects Off, Rotate (one pattern family at a time), or Preset (a complete randomly selected Generate recipe). Both use the same Patterns clock. Preset skips combinations sharing the current three algorithms, including the two Garden variants when already using Garden/Breathe/Blend. The choice is saved with the song.

### Pitch memory in GLO → Generate

K2 selects Locked Loop, Drift Loop or Markov Walk. PRESETS also offers Night Circuit, Kiln Drift and Blue Changes. Preview a recipe, then press REC to accept it. While playing, the shared Generate settings change at the next four-bar boundary.

Locked Loop repeats eight attacks relative to the harmony. Drift Loop replaces an entry on about 16% of attacks. Both can remember percussion choices. Markov Walk listens to the first sixteen generated pitches per melodic layer, then draws from learned one- and two-note transitions within the current scale. The chosen rhythm and interplay still place the attacks. Manual sequences retain their own notes.

The Generate browser has 20 distinct combinations across the 18 factory songs and the additional standalone presets. Garden uses Garden Original. Both Mars songs use Mars Symphonies. Both Analog Days songs use Analog Days. Night Wire uses Night Circuit, Blue Hour Acid uses Blue Changes, and Kiln Echoes uses Kiln Drift. The other song-derived entries carry their song names (Cold Desert Dunes is abbreviated on the display). Recalling one here applies only Rhythm, Phrase, Interplay and Variation.

On GLO → Generate, SAVE opens four global user generator slots. SELECT or PRESETS chooses a slot. REC stores the four displayed generator settings. An occupied slot asks for REC again. HOME cancels. During playback the captured settings persist when playback stops. Recall a user slot after the factory recipes with PRESETS, then REC. A playing song applies the recall on the next four-bar boundary.

On HOME, K2 sets Density for the selected target. Master thins the whole
score and a layer thins its own new attacks. At 100% the authored rhythm
plays in full. Existing notes and effect tails finish naturally. Hold HOME
and turn K2 to reach its original sound macro (Echo on Master). On a layer,
turn any HOME knob, then press HOME + LFO to link that control. Set DEPTH
on the LFO page to hear modulation. Each song saves its four layer densities.

GLO → Generate shows a miniature score for the pitch-based phrase modes.
Gaps and slides identify Acid Line (9), three passes of one motif identify
Locked Loop (10), highlighted substitutions identify Drift Loop (11), and
recurring notes with different continuations identify Markov Walk (12).
These diagrams illustrate each rule and stay steady while browsing.
