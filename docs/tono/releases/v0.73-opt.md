# Tono 0.73-opt

- Generate changes the rules for upcoming notes at the four-bar boundary. Held notes keep their gates, envelopes and effect tails when you change rhythm, phrase, interplay or a Generate preset.
- Band-pass is available in Sound → Edit → Filter and Layer FX → Layer Filter. K2 tunes the centre, K3 sets slope and K4 sets width. Low-pass and high-pass use K4 for slope. All three offer 6, 12, 18 and 24 dB/oct.
- Texture → Voices collects Eagle Landed, Radio Check, Moon Address and Subway Voice. Turn PRESETS and press REC to choose the recording. Existing songs keep their sample IDs.
- SOUND shows the starting preset under the patch title and recalls it with the song.
