# Tono 0.74-opt

- On HOME, hold HOME and tap GLO to toggle Auto Travel. A toast shows On or Off. The timing unit and interval are retained. HOME Help lists the shortcut under Performance shortcuts.
- Press FX from GLO and turn SELECT to EQ. Master and layer EQ share the live spectrum, 3-band and Isolator modes, and Low, Mid and High in 0.5 dB steps from −24 to +18 dB.
- HOME + LFO links layer FX cutoff, filter controls, tremolo and EQ parameters. Layer filter cutoff moves in 100 Hz steps above its 80 Hz minimum.
- Auto notifications identify layer role, sound, rhythm, phrase, interplay, Generate preset, chord and travel changes. Layer handoffs show the previous and new sound or role and the transition used.
