# Tono v0.68-opt

- HOME's footer shows how to reach the second bank of performance controls. On Master, hold HOME for Low EQ, Echo, Mid EQ and Tempo.
- HOME + EDIT opens a contextual manual. HOME gets the full set of applicable shortcuts. Control pages explain ranges, modes and choices without repeating the overview.
- Generate describes every rhythm, phrase and interplay option and shows the current choice by name. LFO covers waveforms, rate modes and destination groups. The rest of the manual includes sound engines, sample playback, effects, envelope, patterns, EQ, harmony and Travel.
